BOTTOM LINE
The acquired company spends $7.9M a year across 386 vendors. We can remove ~$1.9M (24%) of that run-rate within 12 months through three actions, with a further ~$0.2M from travel and tail-spend policy. I am asking for approval to start all three now.

WHAT WE FOUND
- Spend is concentrated: Salesforce alone is $3.1M (40%); the top 20 vendors are ~75% of spend.
- Heavy duplication: 11 audit/tax firms, 13 law firms, 11 telecom carriers, 11 office providers, and overlapping sales/marketing tools (HubSpot, Cognism, Lusha, 6sense, Outreach) next to Salesforce.
- Long tail with no control: 208 vendors (54% of the list) are small one-offs - restaurants, events, hotels booked outside Navan, gifts, individuals paid directly. Small in dollars ($0.8M) but a control and audit risk.

TOP 3 SAVINGS (annual run-rate)
1. Salesforce rightsizing and renegotiation - $0.86M. Audit seat usage, remove inactive licences, align edition to actual use, move onto the parent's enterprise agreement at renewal; cap Cloudcrossing consulting.
2. Office footprint consolidation - $0.51M. Move from 11 office vendors to one flexible hub per country; exit duplicate Zagreb and Chennai sites at lease break.
3. Advisor and consultant rationalisation - $0.52M. Move audit/tax to the parent's firm, let deal-related fees (RSM, Houlihan Lokey, Intralinks) run off, and cut ad-hoc consultants by 60%.

TIMELINE
- Days 0-30: Freeze new vendors without procurement approval; pull contracts for the top 30 vendors (renewal and notice dates); start Salesforce licence audit; enforce Navan and corporate card for all T&E.
- Days 30-90: Terminate deal advisors and tail vendors; issue notices on duplicate offices and telecom; appoint single audit/tax firm and approved legal panel.
- Days 90-180: Salesforce renewal negotiation; consolidate SaaS overlaps (HubSpot, Zapier, Trello/Smartsheet, sales-data tools).
- Months 6-12: Office exits complete as leases allow; full run-rate savings achieved.

IMPLEMENTATION
Procurement owns a single vendor master and approval workflow; each department head signs off their vendor list; Finance tracks savings monthly against this baseline.

RISKS AND MITIGATIONS
- Lease and contract lock-in may delay office and Salesforce savings -> confirm notice periods first; negotiate early-exit or sublet.
- Sales disruption from Salesforce changes -> remove only verified-inactive seats; involve Sales Ops; no change during quarter-end.
- Loss of local expertise (country tax/legal) -> keep one approved local firm per country under the parent's lead firm.
- Employee morale from office changes and cut perks -> communicate early; keep core benefits (health insurance untouched).
- Data quality: 62 small vendors (3% of spend) could not be identified with certainty -> confirm with AP before action.

DECISION REQUESTED
Approve the 30-day actions above and a procurement freeze on new vendors effective immediately.
